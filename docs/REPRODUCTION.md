# Reproduction and evidence boundaries

The frozen hwc.py and hfc.py solver modules retain the original implementation. Entry points use repository-relative paths.

## Coefficient campaigns

The run_campaign.py script constructs an isolated layout under runs/NAME/, copies inputs and code, then invokes the original driver. Frozen records are not overwritten.

- examples: additive, variable diffusion, OU, Wick growth, memory, population, quadratic output and Brownian-limit benchmarks.
- cubic: nonlinear coefficient runs.
- oscillator: affine vector solver and H sweep, using stored independent quadrature references.
- nonlinear: Duffing and pendulum coefficient runs and exact-grid MC; potentially expensive.
- bench: solver timings, retaining archived MC timing references.
- tables: principal benchmark tables from stored records.

The original drivers record fixed seeds, tolerances and parameters. A full campaign is not part of the smoke test.

## New coupled pendulum check

Standard fBm, H=0.9, T=10, sigma=0.4; omega=1, zeta=0.3, Q0=1, V0=0. Coarse increments are sums of a fine Davies--Harte sample. Heun discretizations with 1000, 2000 and 4000 steps share every random path. Outputs at t=1,2,5,10 include moments, marginal sampling standard errors and paired standard errors for differences. Seed and chunk size are part of exact reproduction.

Paired grid agreement is a discretization diagnostic, not a rigorous continuum-bias bound. The original degree-one discrepancy is retained as a baseline; the v1.1.0 degree experiment is documented in PENDULUM_REFINEMENT.md.

## Plotting versus recomputation

The figure script reads released arrays and the closed-form Gram implementation. It generates the current main/supplementary figures and checks data selection. Some comparison curves exist as archived plotted coordinates; their full historical campaigns are not regenerated by this script.

The original experiments and release checks used different software environments. requirements-tested.txt records the latter. Numerical comparisons use tolerances; bitwise equality across platforms is not assumed. The new coupled check was fully executed for this release. The other expensive campaigns are supplied but not all were rerun during packaging.

The v1.1.0 higher-degree pendulum, quadrature-refinement and same-mode MC campaigns were executed. The small rerun is part of verification; expensive higher-degree campaigns are supplied with their complete records and are not repeated by the smoke test.
